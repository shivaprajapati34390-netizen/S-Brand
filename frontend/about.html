ABOUT

One search. Every Indian fashion brand.

S-Brand exists because comparing prices across brands used to mean opening fifteen tabs. Now it's one search.

WHAT WE ARE

A comparison platform, not a store.

S-Brand doesn't manufacture, stock, or ship clothing. Every product shown on this site belongs to the brand it's listed under — Mecnex, Offduty, Gritstones, and the rest of our 17 partner brands. When you buy something, you're buying directly from them.

What we do is pull live price and stock data from each brand's own store, so you can see it all in one place instead of checking each site individually.

HOW IT WORKS

Type it, or show it to us.

  • 01 Type a clothing item, or upload a photo of something you like.
  • 02 We search live across every partner brand's store at once.
  • 03 You see real prices and real stock status, side by side.
  • 04 Tap through to buy directly from the brand — checkout happens on their site.
HOW WE MAKE MONEY

We earn a small commission — you never pay more for it.

Some links on S-Brand are affiliate links. If you buy something after clicking through, we may earn a small commission from the brand — at no extra cost to you. The price you see is the same price you'd pay by going to the brand's site directly. This is how we keep the comparison free for everyone who uses it.

We only link to brands whose stock data we pull directly from their own store — nothing is faked or estimated.
