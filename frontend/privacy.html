Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

This policy explains what information S-Brand collects when you use our comparison platform, how we use it, and who we share it with. We built S-Brand to need as little personal data as possible — there's no account or sign-up required to use it.

2. What we collect

  • Search queries — the text you type or the photo you upload to search for a clothing item.
  • Uploaded photos — processed temporarily by an AI service to identify the garment in the image. We do not build a permanent library of user-uploaded photos.
  • Basic technical data — standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited), used only for keeping the site running and secure.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, address, or payment details at any point — you never enter any of that on S-Brand, since all purchases happen on the brand's own website.

3. How photo search works

When you upload a photo, it is sent to a third-party AI vision service to identify the garment (type, color, style) and generate a short search term. The photo is used only for this purpose. We do not use uploaded photos for training, advertising, or any purpose beyond returning your search results.

4. Live brand data

To show you prices and stock, S-Brand fetches public product data directly from each partner brand's own online store in real time. This is one-way — we read publicly available product information; we do not share your search activity with the brands themselves.

5. Affiliate tracking

Some outbound links on S-Brand are affiliate links, routed through an affiliate network so we can earn a commission if you make a purchase. These networks may use cookies or tracking parameters to attribute the sale back to S-Brand once you land on the brand's website. This tracking happens on the brand's site, governed by their own privacy policy and the affiliate network's policy — not by us directly.

6. Third-party links

S-Brand links out to many independent brand websites. Once you click through, you're subject to that brand's own privacy policy and terms. We have no control over, and are not responsible for, how those sites handle your data.

7. Data retention

We do not maintain user accounts or long-term profiles. Search queries and uploaded photos are used to generate your results and are not retained for longer than operationally necessary.

8. Children's privacy

S-Brand is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as S-Brand evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top of this page.
